Intended use & limitations. Echo-HF estimates the 5-year probability of progression to symptomatic heart failure among individuals without current symptomatic HF, using variables from a standard transthoracic echocardiogram. It is a research tool for risk stratification and is not a diagnostic device; it does not establish that acting on the estimate improves outcomes. The reported estimate is on the Kaplan-Meier scale used in the source study; as with other survival-based risk tools, it does not account for the competing risk of non-HF death. Estimates are least reliable at the extremes of the score range (in internal validation, mean predicted risk in the highest band exceeded observed incidence, 30.3% vs 19.9%) and in populations that differ substantially from the development (KNUH) and validation (ARIC) cohorts. Clinical decisions remain the responsibility of the treating clinician.
Model provenance: this calculator runs the trained Echo-HF XGBoost model (100 depth-1 trees) exactly, embedded directly in this page — no server, no external library. The absolute-risk mapping uses the Cox recalibration fitted on the KNUH cohort.
